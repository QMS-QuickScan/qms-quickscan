Common questions

Before you get in touch.

Is this a certification or compliance audit?

No. It is a limited-scope, independent documentation review. It does not provide certification, legal advice, regulatory approval or a guarantee of compliance.

What documents do you need?

That depends on the agreed review area. We define the document list and acceptance criteria before the engagement. Please do not send confidential or sensitive files before secure handling arrangements are agreed.

Can you help with an urgent audit?

Possibly, depending on the scope, deadline and available capacity. Contact me with the date and objective; availability is not assumed until confirmed in writing.

How do we start?

Send a short description of your company type, the documentation area you want reviewed and your target timing. An initial discussion will establish whether the QuickScan is a suitable fit.
